# New Chapter v6.1 — Internal factual and image source register
Review date: 10 November 2026 (Singapore). Keep this file internal to the project; official URLs must be reviewed again before commercial publishing or advising individual applicants.

## Official Singapore sources
1. MOE, international student admissions: https://www.moe.gov.sg/international-students
   Supports: Schools have separate admission routes; international admissions differ by school level. No guarantee of placements.
2. ICA, Student's Pass for government/government-aided/independent schools: https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/student-s-pass/student%27s-pass-application-renewal/government--government-aided--independent-schools
   Supports: School offer; requirement of Student's Pass for relevant foreign students; exemptions for certain pass holders.
3. ICA, parent/grandparent accompanying a Student's Pass holder: https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/long-term-visit-pass/long-term-visit-pass-application-renewal/parent-or-grandparent-of-a-child-or-grandchild-studying-in-singapore-on-a-student%27s-pass
   Supports: One parent/grandparent per student; eligible local SC/PR sponsor aged 21+; local sponsor submits with Singpass; documents and translation requirements.
4. ICA, family LTVP eligibility: https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/long-term-visit-pass/long-term-visit-pass-application-renewal
   Supports: LTVP family categories differ; eligibility and sponsorship vary by applicant category.
5. ICA, PR eligibility and application: https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/citizenship-and-permanent-residence/permanent-residence
   Supports: Eligible PR categories (family, qualifying work pass, qualifying student, investor). ICA explicitly does not affiliate with migration agencies.
6. ICA, citizenship eligibility: https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/citizenship/
   Supports: Separate citizenship eligibility categories, PR residency conditions where applicable; National Service consideration.
7. ICA, acceptable translations: see parent accompanying Student's Pass holder guidance above. Requirements vary; check case specific instructions.
